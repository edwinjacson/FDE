# Halden Logistics Hybrid and Remote Working Policy
Last updated: 2 September 2024

## Office attendance
Our working model is hybrid. Employees are expected to be in the office a minimum of 3 days per week. Teams agree which days together so that collaboration days overlap. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 regardless of location.

## Home office equipment
Each employee may claim a one-off home office equipment allowance of £250 (desk, chair, monitor or similar). The allowance can be used once every 3 years. Receipts must be submitted to Finance.

## Working abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. This needs prior written approval from the line manager and sign-off from IT Security. The company VPN must be used at all times, and employees must not work from countries on the restricted list held by IT Security.

## Right to work
Working abroad for longer than 20 days may have tax and immigration consequences and needs approval from HR and Finance.
