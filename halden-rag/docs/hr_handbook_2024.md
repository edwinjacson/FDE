# Halden Logistics Employee Handbook 2024
Last updated: 12 January 2024

## 1. Working hours
Standard hours are 37.5 per week, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are set by each department. Overtime must be agreed in advance with a line manager.

## 2. Sick pay
Employees receive full pay for the first 10 days of sickness absence in a rolling 12 months, after which Statutory Sick Pay applies. A fit note is required after 7 calendar days of absence. Employees must tell their line manager before 9:30am on the first day of absence.

## 3. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per holiday year, plus UK bank holidays. The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Up to 5 days may be carried over into the following year and must be used by 31 March. Leave requests should be submitted at least two weeks ahead for periods of five days or more.

## 4. Parental leave
Parental leave is handled in line with statutory entitlements. Employees should speak to HR as early as possible to discuss dates and paperwork.

## 5. Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Concerns can be raised confidentially with HR or through the whistleblowing line.
