# Halden Logistics Expenses Policy
Last updated: 3 June 2024
Owner: Finance

## 1. General principle
Employees may claim reasonable, business-related expenses. Expenses must be necessary, proportionate and supported by evidence.

## 2. Meals
The daily meal allowance when travelling for work is £25 in the UK and £40 internationally. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 3. Accommodation
Nightly hotel caps are as follows:

| Location       | Nightly cap |
|----------------|-------------|
| London         | £180        |
| Other UK       | £120        |
| International  | £150        |

Bookings above the cap need prior approval from the Finance Director.

## 4. Receipts
A receipt is required for any single expense over £10. Expenses of £10 or less can be claimed without a receipt but must still be listed.

## 5. Submitting claims
Claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Claims submitted later than 60 days need Finance Director approval and may be refused.
