Q: How much holiday do I get?
A: 25 days plus bank holidays. The holiday year is Jan-Dec so if you start mid-year you get a pro-rata amount.

Q: Do I have to come into the office?
A: Mostly yes - we expect 4 days a week in the office, 1 from home. Speak to your manager if that's a problem.

Q: What laptop will I get?
A: A standard-issue laptop which is refreshed every 3 years. Ask in #it-help on Slack if yours is slow.

Q: Who do I ask for IT help?
A: #it-help on Slack, or email the service desk. Do not ask in #general, they will not see it.

Q: Where do I find my payslip?
A: In the HR portal under "My pay". Payday is the last working day of the month.
