# Halden Logistics Employee Handbook - 2025 Update
Last updated: 6 January 2025

This update replaces Section 3 (Annual leave) of the 2024 handbook. All other sections of the 2024 handbook remain in force.

## 3. Annual leave (revised)
From 1 January 2025, full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per holiday year, plus UK bank holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount. Up to 3 days may be carried over into the following year and must be used by 31 March. Any days above the carry-over limit are lost.

Leave requests of five days or more should still be submitted at least two weeks ahead.
