# Vendor Management Policy
Last updated: 8 August 2024
Owner: Procurement

## Onboarding
Every new vendor must complete the security questionnaire before any contract is signed. Vendors that will process personal data must also sign a data processing agreement.

## Approvals
Contracts with a total value over £50,000 need approval from a director before signature. Contracts under £10,000 can be approved by a department head.

## Payment
Standard payment terms are 30 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Any request for shorter terms must be approved by Finance.

## Reviews
Vendors classed as critical are reviewed annually. Reviews cover security posture, service levels and financial stability.
