# Halden Logistics Information Security Policy
Last updated: 20 May 2024
Owner: IT Security

## 1. Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems. Passwords must not be shared or stored in plain text; use the approved password manager.

## 2. Devices
Only company-issued devices may be used to access company data. Personal devices must not be used for company data. USB storage devices are banned; use the approved file sharing platform instead.

## 3. Lost or stolen devices
Any lost or stolen device must be reported to IT Security within 2 hours of the loss being discovered, whatever the time of day. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Employees must also report a loss to their line manager.

## 4. Access reviews
Access rights to production systems are reviewed quarterly by system owners. Leavers' access is removed on their last working day.

## 5. Phishing
Suspicious emails should be reported using the "Report phishing" button. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.
